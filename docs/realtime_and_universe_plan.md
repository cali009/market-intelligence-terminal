## Consequence for the "add delisted names" choice

That option is not achievable from this data source. Finding 5 says the names are absent;
finding 6 says the ones that do come back are the *wrong security*, which is worse than
absent because it would silently inject an ETF's price series under a delisted issuer's
ticker. So:

- The survivorship bias in the universe **stays**, and `universe_note` must keep saying so.
- Fixing it needs a vendor with point-in-time constituent history (CRSP, Sharadar,
  Compustat) — all paid.
- A symbol-identity gate becomes mandatory for any universe expansion, not optional.

## Phase A — make staleness honest  *(small, ship first)*

The current badge implies a fault where there is none.

1. Add an exchange-calendar helper (TSX and NYSE sessions + holidays) and publish
   `market_state` per feed: `OPEN`, `CLOSED_WEEKEND`, `CLOSED_HOLIDAY`, `PRE_MARKET`,
   `AFTER_HOURS`.
2. Badge logic: when the exchange is closed, read *"As of Fri 20 Nov close · market closed ·
   next refresh Mon 21:30 UTC"*. Reserve `STALE` for the case that is actually a fault —
   the market was open and we have no close for it.
3. Fix the FX field-name inversion found earlier: `spot_cad_usd = 1.4298` and
   `spot_usd_cad = 0.6994` are labelled backwards. Rename to `spot_usd_per_cad` /
   `spot_cad_per_usd` and update both consumers.
4. New check in `honesty_checks.py`: no feed may report `STALE` for a date the exchange
   was closed.

**Gate:** checks 1–10, page-load harness, full pytest.

## Phase B — universe 19 → 164

1. **Symbol identity gate, first.** Every `security` row gains `expected_name` and
   `expected_exchange`. `sync_security_bars` compares Yahoo's `longName` and
   `fullExchangeName` against them and **refuses to write bars on mismatch**, recording a
   `SYMBOL_IDENTITY_MISMATCH` row instead. Rationale is finding 6.
2. Seed the 145 constituents from `data/universe/index_constituents_2026-11-22.json`,
   with the `-` suffix mapping for the 7 multi-class tickers. Source and fetch date
   recorded in the file.
3. Backfill 5y of daily bars for new symbols (Yahoo serves full history for `1d`).
4. EDGAR CIK lookup for the 94 new US issuers; Canadian issuers get
   `coverage_status = NOT_COVERED_NO_EDGAR` rather than an invented P/E.
5. Rebuild the `factor_risk.py` baskets from real GICS sectors. If that is deferred, the
   factor panel must be labelled "computed on a fixed 19-symbol legacy basket set" —
   finding 11.
6. Time every pipeline stage and publish the totals, so a 164-symbol CI run that quietly
   takes 40 minutes is visible rather than discovered later.
7. Update `universe_note`: 164 currently listed constituents, still survivorship-biased,
   with the reason stated.

**Gate:** checks 1–10 plus a new check that no `security` row has bars written under a
mismatched identity; page-load harness; full pytest; CI runtime within budget.

## Phase C — intraday refresh, labelled delayed

1. A **separate** workflow, not the daily pipeline: `intraday_quotes.yml`,
   cron `*/15 * * * 1-5` guarded to 13:30–21:00 UTC, fetching `interval=5m` for the
   universe and writing one small `intraday_quotes.json`, deploying that file only.
2. The analytics feeds stay end-of-day. Intraday prices are a quote strip, not an input
   to scoring — mixing them would put delayed ticks into features that assume closes.
3. Header reads *"delayed ~15 min · unofficial source · last tick 14:32 ET"* and the age
   counts from the tick, not from the close.
4. Finding 3 is stated in the UI: this is best-effort, not a guaranteed cadence.

## Phase E — true real-time, US only  *(needs two actions from you)*

1. A Firebase **Cloud Function** on a schedule polls Finnhub, serves
   `/api/rt/quotes.json` with a short cache TTL, and keeps the key server-side. Finding 2
   rules out calling either provider from the browser.
2. Page polls every 30–60 s. Canadian symbols stay end-of-day and are labelled as such —
   finding 9 says there is no free legal real-time TSX feed.
3. **You would need to:** move the Firebase project to the Blaze plan, and create a
   Finnhub key. At this volume the function cost is effectively nil, but it is a billing
   change, and the free key's licence is personal non-commercial — a public page
   displaying it is arguably redistribution, which Finnhub reserves for paid plans.
   That risk is yours to accept or not.
